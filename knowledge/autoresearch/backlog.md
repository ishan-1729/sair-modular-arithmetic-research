### Explicit exclusions from the submission research queue

- Recursive Language Models, lambda-calculus RLMs, recursive agent harnesses, REPL agents, and multi-agent voting are orchestration methods, not trained submission architectures. The official sandbox has no network and the submission must be self-contained, so they are excluded from inference experiments.
- Hand-programmed or compiled Transformer execution of a modular-arithmetic program, interpreter, or solver violates the trained-parameters boundary even if implemented inside attention weights. The allowed transfer from programmable-computer work is architectural: train the execution transition, registers, addressing, and readout from data, preserve full initialization provenance, and require causal memory and weight-dependence evidence.
- Do not directly reproduce the pixel/video realization of Neural Computers for this CPU arithmetic submission. Its broader learned-runtime hypothesis is promoted through EXP-646 and EXP-654; the transferable object is persistent learned state and memory/I/O organization, not GUI frame generation. LimiX-style general structured-data foundation models remain comparison-only unless a compact implementation yields a matched arithmetic hypothesis.
- ARC-AGI headline scores are not comparable evaluation evidence for SAIR. HRM/TRM-family papers enter the queue only through mechanisms that can be tested on exact held-out-prime modular rollouts.
