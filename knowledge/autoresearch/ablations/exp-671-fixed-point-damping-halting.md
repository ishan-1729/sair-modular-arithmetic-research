# EXP-671 - Fixed-Point Reasoner damping and residual halting

EXP-671 implemented the frozen bounded SAIR transfer: a shared PreNorm Conv1d refinement with an
exact-zero learned per-channel residual scale, proposal and legal-input reinjection on every call,
six-call training split into two-step TBPTT windows with three deep losses, finite FPR damping, and
target-free normalized-infinity-residual halting with patience two, decay 0.5, minimum depth two, and
hard maximum six. Full, stabilization-fixed, damped-fixed, halt-without-damping, plain-fixed,
proposal-only, reset, and randomized routes were all exercised.

The final package boundary was green: 15 focused tests, project validation at 187 concepts and 1,100
benchmark records, and all 600 tests in 645.78 seconds. The exact child command exited zero; its outer
capture wrapper returned one only after PowerShell expanded the wrapper's final status variable to blank.
The child log and `/usr/bin/time` record are retained, and the oracle was not rerun.

Root `20261234` completed the full frozen 400-update training for each of the three trained arms and
every calibration route. Validation selected threshold 0.1: all seven candidates scored 81.25% on the
32-case validation-only W128 rollout set, while thresholds at least 0.005 exited after two calls instead
of six. This is threshold calibration, not scientific accuracy evidence.

The resource route took 642.353 wall seconds, 2,507.078 aggregate process-tree CPU seconds,
2,853.840 cgroup CPU seconds, and 648,990,720 bytes peak RSS. Complete routes, reload, determinism,
split separation, target-free thresholding, memory, and candidate runtime all passed. Candidate inference
projected to 220.352 seconds nominal and 275.440 seconds with 1.25x headroom, below the 285-second
ceiling. The complete formal route projected to 22,030.105 wall seconds, above the frozen 21,600-second
expected-value ceiling; its ETA was 27,537.632 seconds, soft guard 34,423, and hard guard 55,076.

T066 therefore parks this exact package at resource admission. Roots `20261235`-`20261237`, complete
formal W128, component/reliability gates, W256, and confirmations remain unopened. This is not a
scientific rejection of FPRM, ST-M027, or the source paper, and it changes no candidate, benchmark,
submission, promotion, or Goal-completion evidence.
