# EXP-667 — RSM shallow train / deep inference

Preregistered bounded-faithful test of ST-M016.a. The package compares the full two-state shared-operator route with one-state, deep-supervision, full-gradient, fixed-depth, high-reset, low-reset, proposal-only, and randomized controls.

Root 20261218 completed every route and split. Integrity, determinism, reload, separation, memory, and expected-value checks passed. Nominal candidate inference projected to 244.98 seconds, but the measured 1.60085 variability multiplier raised headroom to 392.17 seconds against the frozen 285-second ceiling. The package therefore parked before root 20261219. Calibration exactness uses tiny denominators and two updates per trained route; it is not scientific evidence.
