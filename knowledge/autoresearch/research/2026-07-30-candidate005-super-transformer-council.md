# Candidate005 super-transformer council

## Evidence boundary

Observed project evidence: Candidate005 retains 97.4% Tier-6 exact accuracy but its structural fallback limits independent overall accuracy to 59.3%. EXP643 found W256 transition accuracy near 99.70-99.75% while autonomous rollout was only 22-32%, so rare errors compound. EXP618 found that replacing hard state with continuous state reduced W128 rollout by 2.6 points. EXP654's de-novo addressable executor was zero-exact at transfer and established no causal useful memory. EXP688 was parked because guarded runtime was 300.913s against 285s. EXP689 supplies h48 feasibility structure only, never a checkpoint or accuracy denominator.

## Source mechanisms versus SAIR adaptations

General architecture mechanisms are inspiration, not local evidence. Every SAIR adaptation retains a hard learned residue, ordinary learned parameters, exact offline labels only, and an input-only deployed route.

## Interpretation discipline

C1 executes first only because it has the lowest council identifier. It is not a selected winner. A resource park, scientific rejection, or positive result for C1 cannot cancel, weaken, merge, or reorder C2-C7. Only the later chair may compare terminal evidence across all seven entrants. Route speed is not accuracy, transition accuracy is not rollout accuracy, and public/checked evidence is not private-seed generalization.

C1 tests continuous evidence *beside* the legally required hard residue, not instead of it. Access begins at exact zero; both arms compute and store identical tokens; state is per example/call. Reset, freeze, cross-case permutation, and randomization must erase at least half of any gain under the frozen paired inference rule before persistence is credited causally.
