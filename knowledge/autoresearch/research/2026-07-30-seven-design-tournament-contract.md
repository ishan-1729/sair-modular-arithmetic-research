# Seven-design tournament contract

The tournament contains all seven council designs: C1 persistent tokens, C2 append-only trace, C3 quotient/carry conditioning, C4 rank-factorized dilation adapters, C5 routed antithetic quorum, C6 cycle-factorized distillation, and C7 quotient/carry x token factorial. Identifier order is execution order only. No entrant may cancel another. The chair is recalled only after seven schema-valid terminal receipts.

## Common immutable rung

Each entrant receives a fresh worktree, owner, roots, data identities, and primary/control pair. The first scientific rung is 2,400 updates per trained arm with curriculum `[64,128,256,256]`, horizon 8, matched optimizer/data/RNG, complete 3,000-case W128/W256 transitions, paired W128 rollout, and frozen sequential W256 blocks. No checked/public answers tune architecture, losses, roots, gates, or stopping. No short checkpoint supports scientific rejection.

Before canonical terminal records, an entrant completes actual all-mode smoke, focused semantics/safety/lifecycle tests, and exactly one dynamically budgeted validator/full-pytest package oracle. A failing oracle produces a mechanical park without opening roots. After the oracle there is no source retuning.

## Resource admission

Calibration measures the exact deployed primary path after a warmup and three cold complete 1,100-case routes. If `max/median > 1.10`, two more complete cold routes are mandatory. Admission requires `2 * max(cold wall) <= 285s`, deterministic output, and RSS at most 6.4 GiB; projections cannot pass.

Representative complete units separately measure wall, process CPU, cgroup CPU, and RSS. Expected work is derived from throughput; soft/hard guards are 1.25x/1.75x. They contain runaway execution without reducing updates or denominators. Evidence is cached by checkpoint, code, config, data, evaluator, root, environment, route, and intervention hashes. Monitoring uses ETA and completion markers. After one timeout, one fresh probe may recover or replan; repeated polling is forbidden.

## Scientific gates

The first rung requires healthy control (at least 99% W128 transition), primary W256 transition at least 99.8%, primary W256 rollout at least 50%, invalid at most 1%, at least +5 paired rollout points, and no more than 5 points W128 loss. Entrant interventions must erase at least half the gain. Paired wins/losses/ties use a 99% time-uniform confidence sequence with Holm allocation.

Only complete primary W256 at least 85%, with every common/causal gate passing, opens two confirmations. Only both confirmations open five untouched seeds; each must reach 90%, aggregate 93%, Candidate005 lower tiers remain bit-identical, and fresh 2x runtime admission passes. These are intermediate gates, never private-score or completion claims.

## Chair package

For every entrant the chair receives identities/hashes, checkpoints, work counts, paired predictions, full denominators, confidence evidence, first-error/invalid metrics, causal interventions, randomized-weight evidence, lower-tier parity, reload/determinism, wall/process/cgroup CPU/RSS, root lifecycle, MLflow IDs, limitations, verification, and pushed equality. Missing healthy control, short-rung negatives, unchecked caches, or resource-as-accuracy reasoning invalidates that entrant comparison, not other entrants.
