Across all three comparisons the same picture emerges. Mixers with data-dependent gating and a delta
rule (GDN, and the hybrids that include it) train more stably than vanilla full attention under looping,
and sparse attention, while less expressive, never destabilizes. This motivates the two LT2 instantiations
used in the rest of the paper: LT2-sparse (Looped Hybrid with DSA) when stability is the priority, and
LT2-linear (Looped Hybrid with GDN) when capability is.
3.6. Synthetic Tasks: State-tracking + Recall

recurrent depth, while retrieval favors precise attention to a long history. The first synthetic experiment
we use to probe LT2 puts both pressures on the same task. We follow the state-based recall construction
