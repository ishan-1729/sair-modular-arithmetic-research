2.3. Hybrid LT2: Mixing Mixers Across Depth and Loops
We further explore hybrid architectures in the loop setting. A common practice in hybrid models is to
interleave linear blocks with full-attention blocks to achieve strong language modeling performance while
restoring recall capability [41, 43, 64, 45]. We show that looped transformers open a second axis for such
mixing: beyond varying the mixer along depth, we can also vary it across loop iterations. We explore both
